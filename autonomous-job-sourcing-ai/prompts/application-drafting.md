# Application Drafting Prompt

The application-drafting stage should use the analyzed job information and candidate context to prepare a tailored draft.

A good implementation should:

1. Reflect the actual requirements of the job.
2. Avoid inventing experience or qualifications.
3. Use evidence from the candidate profile.
4. Keep the draft relevant to the specific role.
5. Produce a draft for human review rather than blindly submitting it.
