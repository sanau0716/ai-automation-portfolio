# AI Business Opportunity Finder

An AI-powered business opportunity discovery system built with n8n and Crawl4AI to research businesses and identify potential automation opportunities.

## Project Overview

This workflow automates the process of researching businesses and collecting information that can be used to identify potential AI and workflow automation opportunities.

## Workflow

### Crawl4AI — AI Business Opportunity Finder

Uses web crawling and AI analysis to research businesses, extract relevant information, and identify potential automation opportunities.
